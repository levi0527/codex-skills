# Racket-return capability and experiment progression

Use for physical racket-return tasks, adapting object-specific contact/flight metrics to the active project. This is an evidence-driven planning pattern, not a fixed architecture, numerical recipe or permission to run experiments.

## Name the demonstrated level

1. Real dynamic contact, with correct object/racket identity and event timing.
2. Effective outgoing trajectory: declared speed/direction, net clearance and landing region as applicable.
3. Whole-body safety, balance and recovery over the full episode.
4. Entry from a general preparation state under the intended sensing and control interface.
5. Continuous returns without hidden resets or privileged reinitialization.
6. Generalization to unseen incoming conditions, with declared independence and coverage.

Record evidence and limits at each level. Contact alone, reference-tracking error, proxy rewards and GPU utilization cannot substitute for these outcomes. Final long-range performance remains the goal while early diagnostics can test a narrower mechanism. A reset-assisted single return does not establish general READY entry or continuous play.

## Choose the next discriminator

**Find a reproducible physical witness in a controlled single-object task.** Fix known states and incoming conditions. Separate ideal racket contact/flight feasibility from full-body execution under actual dynamics, joint limits, actuator constraints and balance. An ideal impulse or kinematic overlap does not prove executable whole-body contact. Report search domain, constraints, seeds and budget: a failed finite search does not prove impossibility, and one lucky trajectory does not establish reliability. Seek reproducibility across declared repeats before treating the witness as known solvable evidence.

**Test native-to-READY entry as its own intervention.** Once a witness exists, compare native motion and READY bridging with matched phase, initial velocities, incoming timing, history and relevant controller state. Avoid bundling a new initializer, timing shift and controller change into an architecture claim. A privileged known state can isolate execution feasibility while leaving general preparation entry unproved.

**Verify learning and conditioning on a small known-solvable task.** Audit the learning and control chains in [causal-review.md](causal-review.md). Compare a matched baseline and predeclared controls, measuring actual functional change and task-conditioned response. Distinguish no parameter update, unhelpful learning signal, insufficient exposure, inadequate control authority and a physical feasibility gap before expanding the task or blaming the optimizer.

**Expand only the needed coverage.** Vary position, timing, direction, speed and racket state; then preparation/recovery conditions, perception errors and continuous incoming sequences as evidence warrants. Retain useful continuous experiments and the fixed capability baseline. The ordering may change when existing evidence already settles a stage or a different discriminator is more informative; do not re-probe established capabilities to defer authorized learning.

## Protect validity without blocking learning

Keep five decisions separate: implementation/data correctness; hard protection against severe anomalies; ordinary learning failure; scientific promotion; budget/authorization. Early low return rate from an unadapted policy can be a learning outcome within a valid bounded run. It need not prevent learning or trigger a numerical-fault response. Severe protections remain in force even when success is scarce.

For anomalous contacts distinguish measured physical penetration from FK-only geometric overlap, using actual runtime geometry, contact backend and trajectories. After a severe anomaly stop the affected execution and diagnose candidate mechanisms within a bounded scope. Repairing the protection path does not pass the functional gate. Do not absorb an unbounded succession of patches into the main experiment.

Register physical metrics, evaluation points, promotion and stop-loss rules from the task and baseline before looking at results. Account for every episode termination, timeout, truncation and unknown ending with explicit denominators. A truncated episode with an unobserved outcome is neither an observed failed return nor a successful one; report missingness and any bounds separately.

## Architecture hypotheses after feasibility evidence

Candidate comparisons include explicit task planning plus a persistent action prior and whole-body feedback; an executable teacher with distillation; task RL; and residual control. Treat these as competing hypotheses, not established winners. Disclose each interface's information, compute and action privileges. A small residual requires enough authority to solve the observed gap; it cannot be assumed to bridge a large capability deficit.

The final behavior should select and adapt action primitives according to the incoming object. Pure playback or a wider local workspace can be useful intermediate evidence, but neither alone establishes that conditional capability. Choose the next architecture experiment by the measured bottleneck and its falsifiable prediction, not by a universal sequence or historical hyperparameter.
